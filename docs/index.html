How the spaces are kept

  • Speak from your own experience. What suits one body may not suit another, and no one is asked to practise anything.
  • Let other people’s experience remain theirs. Ask before you advise.
  • What is shared in the private groups stays there. Please don’t screenshot or repost without permission.
  • The spaces are closely tended. Selling, spam and personal attacks are removed.
