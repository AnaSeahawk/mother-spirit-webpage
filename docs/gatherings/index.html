Gatherings

Gatherings

Times to meet in voice as well as in writing.

Mother Spirit meets in conversation every day, and now and then in real time. The community call is free and always will be. Circles and salons are smaller, held by members of the community, and offered for a modest exchange that honours the time of those who hold them.

Open to all

Mother Spirit Community Call

Once a month · free · hosted by Ana Seahawk

An open call for anyone in the community, new or long-standing, to meet, share what has been arising in practice, and ask what has nowhere else to go. Dates aren’t fixed; each call is announced as it approaches.

Circles & salons

Smaller gatherings with limited places. Costs are kept modest and vary with how involved each gathering is. The community call is always free.
