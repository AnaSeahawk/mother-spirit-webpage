Founders

Ana Seahawk & Li Goldragon

We are partners in life and in practice. Yoga, astrology, Ayurveda and Shivambu are, for us, disciplines to live and test in our own bodies rather than subjects to teach from a distance, and we write down what we find as we go. Mother Spirit is where that learning meets everyone else’s. We began gathering in April 2022.
